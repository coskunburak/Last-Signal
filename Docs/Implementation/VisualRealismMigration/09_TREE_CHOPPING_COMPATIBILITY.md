## 1. Audit of Current Local Tree Chopping Architecture (S003 + S004 v2)

Inspection of the uncommitted local files in `Assets/LastSignal/Scripts/Runtime/Gathering/` and `Assets/LastSignal/Scripts/Editor/TreePhysicsAuthoring.cs` confirms that the project already possesses a sophisticated, authoritative physical tree chopping system:

| Stage / Subsystem | Verified Local Implementation (`Assets/LastSignal/Scripts/...`) | Key Invariants & Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Hit Detection & Target Validation** | `Combat/MeleeAttackResolver.cs`, `Gathering/GatheringWorld.cs`, `Gathering/TreeStructure.cs` | Uses real melee `ActiveWindow` ray/sphere cast against trunk collider. Validates `weapon.axe` tool capability, cut height band (`0.9 m ± 0.2 m`), node availability, and `physicsBody` readiness (`PhysicsNotReady` blocks legacy 4-hit disappearance). |
| **2. Localized Damage & Cut Accumulation** | `Gathering/TreeStructure.cs`, `Gathering/ResourceNodeDefinition.cs`, `Gathering/GatheringState.cs` (`TreeCutSnapshot` v2) | First accepted strike locks 1 of **8 radial directions** (`0..7`). Subsequent hits advance penetration across **16 discrete depth levels** (`1/16` to `15/16`, final `0.975`), scaled by `strikeDamage / health * axeEfficiency / cuttingResistance`, axe durability condition, and trunk radius relative to `0.25 m`. Opposite-side strikes cannot advance or reset the notch. |
| **3. Cut Geometry & Stump Separation** | `Editor/TreePhysicsAuthoring.cs` + `com.dgreenheck.openfracture` (`MeshSlicer` / `FragmentData`) | **Zero runtime mesh slicing** (zero runtime CPU spikes or GC allocs). At Editor authoring time, `TreePhysicsAuthoring` extracts bark (`submesh 0`) and foliage (`submesh 1`), slices at `0.9 m` cut height, and generates **128 mesh pairs** (8 directions × 16 depths: `Top-<side>-<level>.asset` and `Stump-<side>-<level>.asset`) with a sloped V-notch cavity. Submesh 0 = Outer Bark (`Bark-Static.asset` with `_LS_FOREST_STATIC` wind disabled); Submesh 1 = Interior Cut Wood (`Wood.mat`). |
| **4. Joint Weakening & Falling Physics** | `Gathering/TreePhysicsBody.cs`, `Gathering/TreePhysicsProfile.cs` | Intact trunk is held upright by a world-connected `FixedJoint` at cut height (`0.9 m`). As depth $d$ increases, remaining circular support area $A$ shrinks, and the **joint anchor shifts to the centroid of the remaining uncut wood opposite the notch** ($\text{offset} = r \cdot \frac{2}{3}(1-x^2)^{1.5} / (\pi A)$). Gravity acting on the tree's elevated COM naturally creates a bending moment toward the notch! Each axe hit also applies a bounded `8 N·s` impulse at the contact point. When PhysX load exceeds `breakForce` ($\sigma_{\text{axial}} \pi r^2 A$) or `breakTorque` ($\sigma_{\text{bend}} \frac{\pi}{4} r^3 A^{1.5}$), `OnJointBreak` fires. |
| **5. Trunk Collision, Audio & Settling** | `Gathering/TreePhysicsBody.cs`, `Gathering/TreeAudioBank.cs`, `Audio/ProductionAudio.cs`, `Noise/GameplayNoiseSystem.cs` | Trunk uses 2 tapered `CapsuleCollider` approximations; stump uses 2 static boxes (base + narrowed upper support so the stump never props up the notched side). `ContinuousSpeculative` CCD, solver `12/6`, max angular speed `8 rad/s`. Primary landing (`impulse >= mass * 0.15`) + max 2 secondary contacts dispatch bounded splinters/dust, 9-slot `TreeAudioBank`, and `TreeChop`/`TreeImpact` noise events to `ZombieAuditoryMemory`. Rest is declared after 2.0 s of contacted motion below `0.08 m/s` and `0.06 rad/s`. |
| **6. Resource Generation & Persistence** | `Gathering/GatheringWorld.cs`, `Gathering/ResourceNodeView.cs`, `Persistence/SaveCodec.cs` | `OnJointBreak` commits a durable fracture receipt (`nodeId:yield:1`). When the trunk settles, a safe-drop query projects the `HarvestLog` `WorldItem`. **Crucial S004 v2 rule:** Harvesting the log pickup **leaves the fallen trunk, crown, and stump permanently visible and collidable** in the world (`standingVisual` is never hidden on depletion). Full mid-notch, mid-fall (pose + velocity), settled, and WorldCell unload/reload persistence is implemented and verified (`181/181` regression tests). |

## 2. Tree Asset Compatibility Contract (For New Realistic Conifer/Deciduous Models)

Because `TreePhysicsAuthoring.cs` uses `OpenFracture.MeshSlicer` at Editor time to slice the bark mesh with two sloped planes at $y = 0.9\text{ m}$, **not every free Asset Store tree model will slice cleanly**. Every new realistic tree candidate (`European Forests — Realistic Trees`, `Poly Haven Fir Tree 01` derivative, etc.) must satisfy the following 10-point contract before being wired into S004:

| # | Contract Requirement | Exact Technical Specification | Failure Symptom if Violated |
|---:|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Strict Submesh Separation (`Bark` vs. `Foliage`)** | `Mesh` on `LOD0` must have **Submesh 0 = Trunk/Branches (Bark)** and **Submesh 1 = Needles/Leaves (Foliage)** (or separate child `MeshFilter` objects for Trunk and Canopy). | `OpenFracture` assigns the cut cross-section cap to `submesh 1`; if needles are merged into the same submesh as bark, the slicer cuts leaves or maps leaf material onto the wood interior. |
| **2** | **Watertight Manifold Lower Trunk ($y \in [0.2\text{ m}, 1.6\text{ m}]$)** | Within the cut band (`0.9 m ± 0.4 m`), the trunk mesh must be a **closed, manifold cylindrical shell** without open vertical UV seams (unwelded coincident vertices), internal intersecting cards, or floating bark strips. | `OpenFracture` fails to triangulate the interior cap (`non-empty interior-cap submesh` check in `TreePhysicsAuthoring` throws, or leaves hollow/missing faces inside the V-notch). |
| **3** | **Readable Mesh Import (`m_IsReadable: 1`)** | The FBX ModelImporter must have `Read/Write Enabled = true` so `TreePhysicsAuthoring` can extract vertices/triangles in the Editor. | Authoring preflight rejects the mesh (`Mesh is not readable`). |
| **4** | **Pivot, Orientation & Unit Scale** | Pivot at ground contact center (`y = 0` at root base), `+Y` axis = vertical trunk axis, uniform scale `(1, 1, 1)` on root, metric units (`1 unit = 1 m`). | Cut plane at $y = 0.9\text{ m}$ misses the trunk or slices diagonally; inertia tensor and COM calculations skew. |
| **5** | **Circular / Near-Circular Cross-Section at $y = 0.9\text{ m}$** | Trunk radius at $y = 0.9\text{ m}$ must be within `0.12 m – 0.65 m` with circularity ratio $\ge 0.65$ and centered around local $(0, z=0)$. | `TreePhysicsAuthoring` circularity/centering validation rejects the cut section, or the narrowed stump support box misaligns with the remaining wood hinge. |
| **6** | **Vertex Budget in Cut Zone** | Lower trunk (`y < 2.0 m`) should have clean cylindrical topology (`12–32` radial segments, `~300–1,500` vertices in total bark mesh). Photogrammetry trunks (`> 20,000` vertices) must be retopologized/decimated before generating 128 notch mesh pairs. | Generating 128 mesh pairs (`Top` + `Stump`) from a 30k-vertex trunk produces a `> 150 MB` asset folder per tree archetype (current Pine S004 folder is ~19 MB for 824 bark vertices). |
| **7** | **Interior Wood Cap Material (`Submesh 1` on Sliced Bark)** | Must use a dedicated realistic PBR cross-section/split-wood material (`Realism_CutWood_Interior.mat` with sapwood rings/grain + normal/roughness) compatible with the active pipeline (URP/HDRP). | V-notch interior looks flat, plastic, or stretched when viewed from 1.5 m FPS camera (`V09`). |
| **8** | **Static vs. Dynamic Wind Keyword (`_LS_FOREST_STATIC`)** | Bark material on the physical `TreePhysicsBody` must support `_LS_FOREST_STATIC` (disabling world-position vertex wind displacement) while canopy foliage uses object-relative or pivot-consistent wind. | World-space wind shader warps the falling trunk geometry away from its `CapsuleCollider` as it rotates 90° to the ground (`R12`). |
| **9** | **LOD Group Behaviour During Cut & Fall** | Intact decorative trees use full 4-tier `LODGroup` (`LOD0–LOD3`). When an eligible S004 harvestable tree is struck or authored as a `TreePhysicsBody`, the physical assembly drives the active sliced `Trunk`/`Stump` `MeshFilter` + intact `Foliage` child mesh smoothly without LOD popping mid-swing. | LOD transition swaps out the notched `MeshFilter` mid-chop, hiding the cut cavity. |
| **10** | **Persistent Identity & Save Compatibility** | Replacing a tree's visual mesh/prefab in a stage must preserve its `PersistentEntityId`, `ResourceNodeView` node ID, and `HarvestManifest` entry. | Old saves fail `SaveValidation` or spawn duplicate trees. |

## 3. Blender / Editor Pre-Processing Pipeline for High-Detail Realistic Trees

If a sourced realistic tree (such as **Poly Haven `fir_tree_01`** or **European Forests**) has photogrammetry root flares or high-poly bark that exceeds ~1,500 bark vertices:
1. **Split Bark into Clean Lower Trunk + Upper Branches (or Decimate Bark to ~1,000–1,500 verts):** Ensure the $y \in [0.4, 1.4]\text{ m}$ segment is a clean 16–24-sided watertight cylinder with welded seam vertices and baked normal maps from the high-poly source.
2. **Validate with `TreePhysicsAuthoring`:** Run `Last Signal → BLD → P01 → S004 → Create Single Tree Physics Stage` on 1 specimen of each new tree species in isolation before scattering it in the Hero Slice.
